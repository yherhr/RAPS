Who we are

At Rural Asset Protection Solutions we're committed to safeguarding your property, livelihood and peace of mind. With six generations of family farming behind us, we understand the realities of livestock loss, pasture destruction, theft and trespass — not as statistics, but as things that have happened on our own country.

That background shapes how we work: practical methods, honest advice, and jobs done the way we'd want them done on our own place.

Community service runs through it

Our team has a strong history of community service — supporting the Rural Fire Service and partnering with Landcare agencies. The fire advice we give comes from genuine fireground and preparation experience, and the land management approach from years of working alongside the agencies that look after this region.

Who we work for

The same crew serves both ends of the spectrum:

  • Local landholders — from hobby blocks to commercial farms, one fox trap to a full program
  • Councils and public land managers — including work with Bathurst Regional Council, Hill End Common and NPWS
  • Land management organisations across forestry, mining and conservation

If it's an asset to you, it's worth protecting

That's the whole philosophy. Whether the asset is a chook pen, a lambing paddock, a home in the trees or a public reserve, the job is the same: understand the threat, deal with it properly, and keep it dealt with.
