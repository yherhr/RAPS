Who we are

This policy covers the website of Rural Asset Protection Solutions (RAPS), Central West NSW. Questions about it can be sent to ruralaps@hotmail.com or by calling 0422 592 095.

Last updated: August 2026.

What we collect and why

The only personal information this site collects is what you choose to give us through the enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address and message. We use it for one thing — responding to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, arranging and delivering the work you've asked about.

We don't run accounts, logins or mailing lists, and we never sell or trade personal information.

Who handles the data

A few service providers help run this site, and your information passes through them only as needed:

  • Web3Forms — delivers enquiry form submissions to our email inbox
  • Cloudflare — hosts the website and keeps standard security and traffic logs (such as IP addresses) to protect the site
  • Google Analytics — may be used to understand how visitors use the site, through cookies; you can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time

Storage and retention

Enquiries arrive as email and are kept as normal business records for as long as they're needed — for example while quoting or completing a job, or as required for tax and business record-keeping. If you'd like an enquiry deleted, ask and we'll remove it where the law allows.

Links to other sites

The site links to our Facebook page. Facebook has its own privacy policy, and what you do there is covered by it, not this one.

Access, corrections and complaints

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or raise a concern by emailing ruralaps@hotmail.com. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
