Vertebrate Pest Management

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) that tackles the whole problem — from rabbits and foxes to pigs, wild dogs, deer and goats.

Book a free consultation

Integrated, not piecemeal

One-off knockdowns feel productive and change little — the survivors breed and the neighbours' ferals move in. Integrated Pest Management means assessing the real pressure on your country, hitting the pests that matter with the methods that work, coordinating beyond your boundary, and monitoring so the numbers stay down.

It's the difference between buying a result and renting one.

Every major pest, one program

RAPS runs targeted control across the full spread of vertebrate pests:

  • Feral pigs — drop gates, trapping and coordinated programs
  • Foxes — from a chook-pen trap to lambing-season programs
  • Rabbits — warren mapping, destruction and follow-up
  • Wild dogs — professional trapping and district coordination
  • Feral deer & goats — whole-mob capture before they strip your country

The drop-gate difference

At the centre of the pig, goat and deer work sits our most effective tool: remote-controlled cellular drop gates — watch the whole mob feed in on your phone, then take the lot in one capture. It's one of the most proven methods going, especially for pigs.

Working in with everyone

Pests move across boundaries, so the best programs do too. RAPS works in with neighbours, Local Land Services and government agencies — the same coordinated approach behind our work for councils and public land managers.
