Wild Dog Control

Nothing ends a sheep enterprise faster than uncontrolled wild dogs.

Book a free consultation

More than the kills

The losses you find are only part of it — mauled survivors, mismothered lambs, stressed stock that won't do, and the constant worry of what tonight brings. Districts that lose the dog fight often lose their sheep flocks entirely.

Professional dog work

Wild dogs are the hardest vertebrate pest there is, and the work reflects that:

  • Sign assessment — tracks, kills, camera evidence — to understand what's actually working your country
  • Professional trapping targeted to travel routes and access points
  • Camera monitoring so effort goes where the dogs are, not where they were
  • Coordination with neighbours, Local Land Services and agencies — dogs are a landscape problem
  • Integration with your fox and pig programs where it makes sense

Acting early matters

One dog scoping the boundary is a very different job to an established pack. If you're seeing sign or losing stock, the free assessment should happen this week, not next month.
