Feral Deer & Goat Control

Growing mobs of feral deer and goats strip feed, flog fences and hammer regrowth — and they're spreading through the Central West.

Book a free consultation

A newer pressure, moving fast

Feral deer numbers in particular have climbed sharply across NSW. Deer compete directly with stock for feed, damage fences and young trees, and are a genuine road hazard. Feral goats do the same while out-breeding almost everything on four legs.

Controlling mobs, not individuals

Because deer and goats run in mobs, the same whole-mob logic as pig work applies:

  • Remote-controlled cellular drop gates — watch the mob feed in, then take the lot
  • Property assessment to map camps, trails and feed pressure
  • Control programs sized to your place and the season
  • Follow-up monitoring so the next mob through doesn't settle in

Protecting feed and fences

Every mouthful a feral mob takes is feed your stock don't get. If you're seeing deer or goat sign regularly, the pressure is already worth measuring — the assessment is free.
