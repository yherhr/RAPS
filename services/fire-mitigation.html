Fire Mitigation

Practical bushfire preparation that gives your property the best chance when it counts.

Book a free consultation

Preparation is the plan

By the time smoke is on the horizon, your options are whatever you prepared earlier. Real fire mitigation is done in the cool months: fuel down, breaks cut, access open, water reachable, and everyone is clear on what happens if.

It's unglamorous work that decides outcomes.

The full fire program

Each piece is a service in its own right — together they're a fire plan:

  • Weed control — cut the fine fuel at its source
  • Vegetation slashing — knock down the fuel bed around assets
  • Fire breaks — strategic breaks and asset protection zones
  • Road & track clearing — escape routes, fire crew access and water points
  • Protection zones, pump and tank readiness, and written emergency planning

Backed by real fire experience

The team's volunteer firefighting background isn't a marketing line — it's why the advice is practical: what holds, what doesn't, and what crews actually need from a property on a bad day.

And the work is done with care for the country: fuel reduction and clearing are planned around natural habitats and restoration areas — the aim is a safer property, not a stripped one.
