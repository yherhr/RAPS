Vegetation Slashing

Slashing is the fastest way to pull the fuel out from under a fire before it starts.

Book a free consultation

Fuel down, risk down

Fire runs on fine fuel — the grass and light scrub standing dry in late spring. Slashing knocks that continuous fuel bed down to a level where fire behaviour changes completely: slower spread, shorter flames, and something crews can actually defend.

Where slashing earns its keep

Regular slashing keeps the dangerous zones under control:

  • Asset protection zones around homes, sheds and yards
  • Fire breaks and boundary lines, kept effective season after season
  • Laneways, access tracks and around water points
  • Roadside frontages and high-risk corners of the place
  • Pre-season tidy-ups before the grass cures off

Scheduled, not scrambled

The cheapest slashing is the pass booked before the season, not the emergency job during it. Set a schedule once and the fuel never gets away on you.
