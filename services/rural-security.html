Rural crime is a business cost

Fuel, tools, quads, stock, firearms — rural theft targets exactly the things a property can't run without, and isolation is the thief's best friend. Trespass, illegal hunting and stock interference carry their own costs in biosecurity, liability and plain peace of mind.

Layered security that fits farm life

Good rural security is layers, not gadgets:

  • Remote solar cameras — live views and motion alerts to your phone
  • Gate sensors and early-warning alerts on entry points
  • Stock yard and shed monitoring, with hidden options where warranted
  • Practical hardening — the right locks, lighting and layout advice
  • Help choosing and fitting gear that suits your place, not the catalogue

Stronger together

A connected district is a hard target. RAPS helps set up community safety networks — neighbours sharing sightings and alerts — because the property that gets a warning is the one that doesn't get hit.
