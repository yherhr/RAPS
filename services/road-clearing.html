Road & Track Clearing

When a fire is running, an overgrown track is a track you don't have.

Book a free consultation

Access is a fire asset

Every fire plan depends on movement — getting family out, getting fire crews in, getting to water and stock. Tracks that have narrowed with regrowth, washed out, or grown a canopy of low limbs fail exactly when they're needed most, and they burn like a fuse.

Clearing that opens the place up

RAPS clears and maintains the routes your plan relies on:

  • Fire trail and access track clearing — width, overhead clearance and surface
  • Escape route planning and clearing from homes and work areas
  • Access to dams, tanks and pump sheds for firefighting water
  • Regrowth control so cleared stays cleared
  • General property track maintenance year-round

Walked, planned, cleared

It starts with a drive around your place: where you'd need to move, what's in the way, and what it takes to fix. That look is part of the free consultation.
