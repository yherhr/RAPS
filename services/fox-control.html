Why foxes cost you

Foxes are the quiet earner among the pests — a few percent of lambs here, a raided chook pen there, native wildlife gone without a trace. Lambing losses to foxes are routinely underestimated because the evidence disappears overnight.

Right-sized control

No job is too small — fox work scales from backyard to boundary:

  • A single fox trap set and managed to protect chickens or pets
  • Lambing-season programs timed for when ewes and lambs are most vulnerable
  • Trapping and monitoring targeted to dens, trails and travel routes
  • Coordinated control with neighbours — the district approach that actually cuts numbers

Honest advice first

Sometimes the fix is a better pen; sometimes it's a season-long program. The free assessment tells you which, before you spend a dollar.
