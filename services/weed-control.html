Weed Control

Weeds steal pasture all year — then cure off and become the fuel that carries a fire straight at your assets.

Book a free consultation

Two problems in one plant

Invasive weeds cost you twice. Through the growing season they smother pasture and downgrade country; come summer, tall dry weed growth is exactly the fine fuel that lets a fire build speed and jump breaks. Controlling weeds is production work and fire work at the same time.

Practical weed management

Weed work is matched to the species and the country:

  • Property weed assessment — what's there, how far it's spread, what it threatens
  • Targeted control programs for the problem species on your place
  • Fuel-load reduction around assets, fence lines, tracks and breaks
  • Follow-up treatment, because one pass rarely finishes a weed
  • Advice on keeping on top of it between visits

Tied into your fire plan

Weed control, slashing and breaks work together — done as one program, each makes the others cheaper and more effective. Ask about bundling them in a fire mitigation plan.
