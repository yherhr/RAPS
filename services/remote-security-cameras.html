Eyes on the place, 24/7

Rural crime works on one assumption: nobody's watching. Solar-powered cellular cameras break that assumption — no mains power, no Wi-Fi needed, just coverage where your entry points and assets are, streaming to your phone with motion alerts the moment something moves.

Set up properly, not just sold

The difference is in the install:

  • Site assessment — entry points, blind spots, cellular coverage, sun for solar
  • Quality camera systems chosen for your property, not whatever's on special
  • Mounting, power and configuration done right the first time
  • Phone app set up and you trained on it before we leave
  • Service checks so the system's working when you finally need the footage
