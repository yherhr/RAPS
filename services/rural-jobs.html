The jobs in between

Not everything fits neatly under pest, fire or security. Country properties run on a hundred practical jobs — the fence the pigs flattened, the mob that needs shifting, the yard that needs a hand. RAPS does that work too, with the same gear and the same attitude.

What we take on

If it's practical rural work, ask — the usual run includes:

  • Fencing — repairs after feral or fire damage, exclusion fencing, general farm fencing
  • Stock transport — local livestock cartage done calmly and on time
  • Small protection jobs — a fox trap for the chook pen, netting a garden, securing a shed
  • Property odd jobs that need machinery, a ute and someone reliable
  • Larger complex contracts combining several of the above

One call does the lot

Because RAPS already handles your pest, fire and security work, adding the practical jobs means one operator who knows your place instead of five who don't.
