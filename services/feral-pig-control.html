Feral Pig Control

Pigs tear up pasture, wreck crops, foul water points and take lambs — and after a good season their numbers explode.

Book a free consultation

The damage adds up fast

A mob of feral pigs can turn a paddock over in a night. Beyond the visible rooting, they compete with stock for feed, damage fences and dam banks, prey on lambs, and can carry disease. Left alone, a small family group becomes a district problem within a couple of seasons.

How RAPS tackles pigs

The approach is matched to the pressure on your place:

  • Remote-controlled cellular drop gates — our most effective method, capturing whole mobs in one go
  • Conventional and automatic (APS) trapping where it suits the site
  • Pre-feeding and camera monitoring so control happens when it counts
  • Coordination with neighbours and agencies, because pigs don't respect boundary fences
  • Follow-up monitoring so a knockdown doesn't quietly rebuild

Small blocks to big country

Whether it's a few pigs hitting a hobby block or sustained pressure across a large holding, the free on-property assessment works out what's actually moving through your country before anything is deployed.
