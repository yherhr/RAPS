Rabbit Control

A handful of rabbits is a warren by spring — and bare, eroding country a season after that.

Book a free consultation

Small pest, big cost

Rabbits out-eat sheep hectare for hectare, ringbark young trees, undermine sheds and dam walls with their digging, and open the door to erosion on light country. Because the damage builds up slowly, most people leave it a season too long before acting.

Getting on top of them

Effective rabbit work targets the warren, not just the rabbit:

  • Property inspection and warren mapping so the real population is known
  • Control programs matched to your country and stocking
  • Warren destruction where appropriate — remove the home and the numbers stay down
  • Follow-up monitoring, because reinvasion from neighbouring country is the normal pattern

Protecting the long game

Rabbit control pays off in pasture, tree survival and soil held together. It's also one of the cheapest pest problems to fix early and one of the dearest to fix late.
